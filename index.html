How to play

  • Move: Arrow keys (or WASD). On mobile: on-screen arrows.
  • Nap: Hold Space (mobile: “Nap”) while on the pink towel to drop your Ty Ty.
  • Post: Press E (mobile: “Post”) when you’ve picked up a 📱 “TikTok idea”.
  • Collect: ☕️ (cof cof), 🍣 (sush), 🍧 (Zoopy Doopy), 🧀, 🍷 add Good-Times.
  • Time: The day lasts 120s. Max vibes, more followers, minimum Ty Ty.
